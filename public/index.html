CATEGORY 02 • OPERATIONAL CONTEXT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

Why We Built Vernds AI for 14566 Helpline

Understanding the National Helpline Against Atrocities (14566), who calls it, what operators do, and how AI saves lives during extreme trauma emergencies.

What is 14566?

14566 is the Toll-Free National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA) operated under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE), Govt. of India, for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST).

Who Calls 14566?

SC/ST victims facing severe caste discrimination, mob violence, land displacement, social boycott, physical assaults, or immediate life-threatening danger under the PoA (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989.

Human Operator Challenge

In extreme trauma, callers stutter, cry, or speak in regional dialects. Human operators take 15–20 minutes to evaluate fear levels manually, causing critical response delays.

The Vernds AI Solution

Automated 20ms Voice Biomarker Triaging & Instant Dispatch

Vernds AI silently listens during 14566 call streaming. In 20 milliseconds, it measures vocal tremor jitter, pitch instability F0, and speech pause ratio (>35%), extracts village location addresses via NLP NER, calculates standardized SVI score (0-100), and auto-triggers 112 Police ERSS payloads & Tele-MANAS doctor bridges in under 3 seconds!
